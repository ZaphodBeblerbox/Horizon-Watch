# Required by sar_detector.py (AllenAI Sentinel-1 vessel model) and
# satlas_superres.py (Satlas ESRGAN). Both import it lazily inside their
# functions, so the app boots and every other feature works without it —
# which is exactly why its absence went unnoticed: torch was NEVER declared
# here, so on Railway the SAR detector has been failing at call time with an
# ImportError while the service looked healthy.
#

# that are hundreds of megabytes of dead weight on a CPU-only container and
