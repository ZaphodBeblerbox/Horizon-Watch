# Russian hybrid / grey-zone targeting in Europe: what ship-launched drones go after

Research brief for Parallax (defensive OSINT monitoring). Compiled 2026-10-11 from open sources. Each claim is tagged with a source number [S#]; the full list is at the end. No classified material was used. Most attribution here comes from governments, think-tank datasets and court cases, and it varies a lot in strength. The **Conf.** column grades each one.

---

## 1. Executive summary

1. **The hypothesis is only partly supported.** Russian hybrid activity is cheap, deniable and aimed at fear and disruption. Seemingly random soft targets do appear, but almost only in **proxy arson** (a shopping centre, IKEA, Prague buses, warehouses) and in **symbolic information stunts** (coffins at the Eiffel Tower, red hands on the Holocaust memorial) [S7][S9][S28][S29].
2. **For drones, which is the threat Parallax watches, the evidence points the other way.** The largest dataset (IISS, 144 incursions, Aug 2024–Feb 2026) breaks down as about **48% military facilities, 26% critical infrastructure (ports, energy, industry) and 18% civil airports**. There is no documented stadium, venue or shopping-centre case [S1][S2][S3].
3. Military targets cluster on **NATO nuclear-sharing air bases** (Kleine-Brogel, Volkel, Lakenheath), **naval and submarine bases** (Île Longue, Eckernförde/Kiel, the carrier *Charles de Gaulle*), Ramstein, and **sites where Ukrainians train or Ukraine aid is staged** [S1][S2][S3].
4. The purpose looks **instrumental, not symbolic**. IISS lists four aims: reconnaissance-by-provocation (radar, frequencies, rules of engagement), mapping of infrastructure (especially nuclear-sharing and Ukraine-logistics sites), economic and psychological attrition, and making airspace violations normal [S3].
5. The **Ukraine support chain** is the strongest single thread across every method: the DHL air-cargo test parcels, Leyton's Ukraine-aid warehouse, Poland's Warsaw–Lublin rail line, the Rzeszów rail-camera network, the Rheinmetall CEO plot, the WB Electronics drone plant, and in **Aug 2026 an explosive FPV drone that hit a Ukrainian An-124 at Leipzig/Halle** [S10][S12][S13][S14][S15][S16][S30].
6. **The campaign is escalating** from overflights to armed attempts: Leipzig in Aug 2026 (explosive drones, which Germany formally blamed on Russia on 1 Sep 2026) and an explosive sea drone near Romania's Neptun Deep gas platform (20 Aug 2026) [S16][S17][S18].
7. **The ship as a launch platform is plausible but rarely proven.** Only one launch has been publicly attributed: Sweden's attribution of the *Zhigulevsk* spy ship in Feb 2026. The *Boracay* (ex-*Pushpa*) tanker's link to Denmark was never charged; its captain was convicted only for disobeying the French navy [S4][S19][S20].
8. **Uncertainty is large.** In June 2026 Copenhagen police said they could neither prove nor rule out that drones were over CPH on 22 Sep 2025. Frankfurt's 2025 closure was a hobbyist. A *Trouw* review of about 60 incidents found "much panic and hardly any evidence". Most Baltic cable breaks were judged probable accidents [S5][S21][S22].
9. **Recommended watch priority near the coast:** military air bases and nuclear-sharing sites, then Ukraine-aid logistics hubs and cargo airports, naval bases, then arms plants, civil airports, ports and energy (LNG, power). Stadiums, venues and shopping centres get a low base weight, raised during major events.
10. **The best precursors** are Russia-linked or shadow-fleet vessels loitering or drifting off-route within about 10–100 km of a site, AIS gaps, flag or name changes, night-time timing, multi-night reconnaissance before an incident, and political or aid-delivery events.

## 4. Synthesis: is "symbolic or meaningless soft target for deniability" supported?

**Where it holds (proxy ground sabotage and information stunts):**
- Soft civilian targets with no obvious military value are well documented: Marywilska, IKEA Vilnius, Prague buses and a planned cinema, Warsaw/Radom DIY warehouses, the Riga museum, Paris memorials and mosques [S7][S8][S26][S28][S29].
- Officials describe the aims as fear, chaos and polarisation (PST, MI5, French courts) [S28][S34][S35].
- The likely reason is the means, not a preference. Telegram-recruited one-time agents can only reach unguarded places, and seemingly random targets keep attribution murky [S27].

**Where it fails (drones, especially ship-launched):**
- About 48% military and 26% critical infrastructure in IISS. Civil airports are 18% and often dual-use (Aalborg) or Ukraine-logistics hubs (Leipzig, Liège, Rzeszów) [S1][S3].
- Named targets are high-value and purposeful: nuclear-sharing bases (Volkel, Kleine-Brogel, Lakenheath), the SSBN base (Île Longue), a carrier, an ammunition plant (Unterlüß), a naval shipyard (TKMS), an LNG terminal (Brunsbüttel), a nuclear plant (Doel), and a Ukrainian An-124 [S2][S3][S16][S41].
- **No documented drone incident at a stadium, concert or shopping centre in Europe** linked to Russia in 2022–2026 (searched; the only stadium drone stoppages were unrelated hobby or political flags) [S52].
- Even the "disruption" incidents (airport closures) work as *reconnaissance by provocation*: measuring detection, response time and rules of engagement. IISS states this explicitly [S3].

**Verdict:** The hypothesis holds for *deniability and psychological effect*, but not for *target meaninglessness* in the drone domain. Seemingly meaningless targets mostly reflect what proxies can reach. A drone watch system should weight military and Ukraine-logistics sites most heavily. Mass-gathering venues are a tail risk worth a time-limited uplift during major events, not a base priority.

**Conflicting assessments to keep in view:**
- Copenhagen police (June 2026) could not prove drones were over CPH [S5]. *Trouw*: "much panic and hardly any evidence" across about 60 incidents [S22]. IISS itself says many sightings were not individually verified [S2].
- Baltic cables: a Washington Post report (Jan 2025, via secondary coverage) said US and European officials leaned toward accidents [S21]. The Eagle S criminal case was first dismissed for lack of jurisdiction, then sent back by the appeal court [S43][S44].
- *Boracay*: the French case covered only disobeying naval orders, never the drones [S20].
- Vilnius balloons were mostly cigarette smuggling. The "hybrid" framing is Lithuania's and is disputed by Minsk [S49].

### Time multipliers (suggested)
- **Summits and VIP visits:** ×1.5 within ±10 days. Danish drones came 9 days before the 1 Oct 2025 EU summit in Copenhagen; Dublin drones came on Zelensky's arrival night [S48][S51].
- **Military exercises and naval port calls:** ×1.5 for the duration. *Charles de Gaulle* was hit during Lafayette 26 [S19].
- **Ukraine aid deliveries:** ×1.5 at the relevant hub. Leipzig coincided with Ukrainian An-124 cargo flights carrying ammunition [S16].
- **Waves:** after any confirmed incident, raise the whole region ×1.3 for about 7 days. The Danish, Norwegian and German waves ran 22–28 Sep 2025, and the Belgian wave included several nights of base overflights before the airport closures [S5][S50].
- **Night:** almost all drone incidents happened in the evening or at night. Weight 18:00–04:00 local ×1.5 [S3][S5].
- **Elections:** these mainly trigger cyber/DDoS (Danish municipal elections, Nov 2025) rather than drones. Apply ×1.2 to government sites [S42].
- **Major mass events (stadium_venue):** raise to about 0.35 during events with international or political symbolism (e.g. big tournaments). This rests on doctrine (fear effect), not on observed cases.

## 6. Precursor indicators (what to watch)

**Maritime (the primary layer for ship-launched drones):**
1. **A Russia-linked or shadow-fleet vessel loitering, drifting or slowing off-route within about 10–100 km of a high-weight site.** *Boracay* was about 80 km from CPH; *Zhigulevsk* about 10 km from the carrier; *Arctica*, *Cgas Leopard* and *Tranquil Sea* sat off the Dutch/French coasts during Volkel; *Lauga* was linked to 19 overflights in a German probe [S3][S4][S19].
2. **AIS gaps or a dark vessel near the incident.** An unidentified vessel without AIS was under 40 km from the Irish naval ship off Dublin [S3].
3. **Flags of convenience, recent name or flag changes, sanctions listing.** *Boracay* = ex-*Pushpa*/*Kiwala*, Benin flag; *Eagle S* → *Tranquil Sea*, Cook Islands flag [S4][S3].
4. **Russian private security contractors aboard** (Moran Security Group on *Boracay*) [S3].
5. **Small coastal freighters calling at local ports during sightings** (*Hav Dolphin* docked in the UK during Lakenheath; *Scanlark* in the Kiel Canal) [S3][S46].
6. **Russian Navy intelligence ships (AGIs) near exercises or port calls** (*Zhigulevsk*) [S19].
7. **Anchor-drag signatures over cables and pipes:** slow speed (< 5 kn) over a mapped cable route, heading wander [S43].

**Air and RF:**
8. **Multi-night reconnaissance before an escalation.** Kleine-Brogel was overflown on several nights before the Brussels/Liège closures; Volkel on ≥3 days [S2][S50].
9. **The same night, several sites along one coast** (the Danish pattern of 24–27 Sep 2025) points to one mobile platform [S5].
10. **GNSS interference spikes** near the target region (Baltic, Kaliningrad) [S40].
11. **Cellular-controlled drones:** Leipzig used SIM/5G control and a relay antenna. RF anomalies and odd antennas near airfields are indicators [S16].

**Human and online:**
12. **Telegram/TikTok job posts** offering cash for photos, graffiti or "deliveries" near infrastructure; the payment ladder runs from about $3,000 to €10,000 [S26][S29][S31].
13. **Test runs:** dummy or GPS-tracker parcels and hidden cameras on rail lines preceded the planned attacks [S10][S13][S45].
14. **Low-level harassment escalates:** vandalism, then arson, then explosives (Poland 2023–2025; Germany 2024–2026) [S13][S15][S16].

**Political and informational:**
15. **Upcoming summits, VIP visits, exercises and aid deliveries** (calendar layer).
16. **Pre-positioned deflection narratives** in Russian state media ("NATO false flag", "Ukrainian provocation") during or right after incidents [S37].

**Damping (to cut false positives):**
- Require a corroborated sighting by radar or two independent sensors before alerting.
- Check hobbyist and drone-club activity near the site (Frankfurt case).
- Separate smuggling balloons (LT/PL border) from drones.
- Treat a single visual report as low confidence (Copenhagen retrospective) [S5][S22][S49].
